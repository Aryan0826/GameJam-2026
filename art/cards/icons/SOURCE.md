# Pixelarticons

Gerrit Halfmann, Pixelarticons 1.8.1 (MIT).

Source: https://github.com/halfmage/pixelarticons/tree/v1.8.1
Archive: https://registry.npmjs.org/pixelarticons/-/pixelarticons-1.8.1.tgz

Original bug, eye, bullseye-arrow and shield SVG paths are unchanged.
Only `currentColor` was replaced with white so Godot can tint the icons.
No AI-generated artwork. Card frames and text are ordinary interface elements drawn in Godot.
